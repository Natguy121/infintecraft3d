Elemental Lab — Infinite Craft in 3D

A crafting game like Infinite Craft, but in 3D with real physics. Walk around a giant lab, grab the four elements (💨 Air, 🔥 Fire, 🌍 Earth, 💧 Water), throw them and smash them together to discover more than 300,000 items: Steam, Life, Dragons, Volcanoes, Robots, Galaxies and more.

▶ Play now, free

How to play Infinite Craft in 3D

  • Grab an element from a pedestal (hold left click, or tap on mobile).
  • Combine by holding it against another item, throwing it hard into one, or pressing E.
  • Discover: Water + Fire = Steam, Earth + Water = Mud, Life + Tree = Bird, Monster + Ice = Yeti…
  • Drop 3+ items into the Fusion Ring for multi-ingredient recipes.

Features

  • 300,000+ discoveries, each built as a real 3D object at real-world size (a car is car-sized, a castle is huge).
  • Physics: everything can be grabbed, thrown, stacked and smashed.
  • Multiplayer with a room code: mix your items with your friends'.
  • Daily challenges, XP and levels, combos, achievements and a Recipe Book with hints.
  • Horror mode: play in a dark lab with a flashlight… and something watching you.
  • Works on PC, phones and tablets. No download or sign-up needed.
